# Calendar guide

Import Career_Roadmap_60_Days.ics into a new calendar after checking the suggested times. Events are tentative and transparent; they do not reserve meetings or book an exam. Europe/Berlin times are converted to UTC, including the October daylight-saving change. Duplicate imports may create duplicate events depending on your calendar application.

The calendar contains study blocks, weekly career activity, gap-action deadlines and monthly policy reviews. All milestones are proposals. No invitations are sent.
